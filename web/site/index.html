Typeset and print hand-bound books, free, from your own files.

Write a manuscript in plain text. Wynkyn lays the book out in your browser, shows the real pages as you write, prints them imposed into folded signatures for binding by hand, and writes a reflowable EPUB.

Open the editor No account. Nothing to install. Works best in Chrome or Edge.

  • Free and open source. MIT licensed, no tiers, no trial.
  • No account. Open the editor and start writing.
  • Your files stay on your device. A book is a folder of plain files that the app reads from your own disk.
  • Print at home. Signatures come out folded and in order, ready to sew. Or export an EPUB for readers.

How it works

A book is a folder: manuscript.md holds the words with a little semantic markup (chapters, epigraphs, poems, footnotes), and metadata.json holds every styling decision. The app keeps them apart, so you never put a font or a page break in your prose.

{chapter: one}

{dropcap}
In my younger and more vulnerable years my father gave me some advice
that I’ve been turning over in my mind ever since.

Styling is done by clicking the page, not by writing CSS. Change the rule for chapter titles once and every chapter follows. The preview is the same layout the printer gets, so what you see is what you fold.
